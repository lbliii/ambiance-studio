# Assemble the animated picture

Inspect the canonical actions, asset identities, saved views and actual `scene timing`. Present motion in each requested composition at normal speed. Judge its established cause/theme and appropriate emphasis as well as readability; stable elements can stay still while justified environmental life continues. Read [motion direction](../../../docs/MOTION-DIRECTION.md) and the [animation workflow](../../../docs/workflows/03-scene-and-animation.md).

Use CLI scene transactions and the shared evaluator for preview/export. Follow the [scene contract](../../../docs/SCENE-CONTRACT.md); the browser supports rig editing and inspection, not final MP4 export. For attachment or registration changes use the [rig workbench](../../../docs/RIG-WORKBENCH.md). Anchor bases, keep mounted subjects on shared rigs and preserve required backing and occluders.

Before tuning motion, use `render rig-proof` for [whole-object isolation/removal](../../../docs/LAYER-AND-LIGHT-PLANNING.md#whole-object-ownership-and-proof), then internal rest/extreme and fixed-occluder comparisons. Hiding a character must remove its still body and animated head; inspect every contributing layer for residual paint. Do not parent production rigs to a removable reference/fallback layer. Keep fixed glass and receiving shadows independent. Judge repeated flames' shape, cadence and phase together; compare supporting motion enabled/disabled when readability is uncertain.

Use `scene clock` for validated loop-duration changes and inspect timing/audio consequences. Actual cel tracks can override fallback cycles. `scene activity --compare` supports held poses and compatible registered cel alternatives at fixed object geometry; shrinking a whole curtain does not reduce its deformation. Unsupported per-cel sockets or driven companions need a coordinated study. See [current operations](../../../docs/MIDNIGHT-OPERATIONS.md).

Use [source bindings](../../../docs/BINDINGS.md) for explicit source-to-follower responses. Attachment, driving and receiving are different relationships. Prepare dim/base artwork and explicit receiver masks; inspect source extremes, low/high/off, isolated illumination and both compositions. Brightness modulation does not establish changing painted silhouettes.

Make a low-resolution motion-and-sound review packet before polishing. Inspect zero, intermediate times, camera extremes, edge coverage, attachments and the actual last-exported-frame to first-frame join. Export N samples from 0 through N−1; do not duplicate the endpoint. State equality at 0/T does not prove raster or encoded quality.

Use `scene check`, paired proofs and actual browser/encoded inspection as distinct evidence. Render captured subjects with `--revision`; never fall back to working inputs. Record real observations with `plan coverage --stage animation` and affected reviews, retaining missing scope and unperformed checks. [Saved views](../../../docs/VIEWS.md) and [captured editions](../../../docs/REVISIONS.md) preserve exact subjects across changes.
